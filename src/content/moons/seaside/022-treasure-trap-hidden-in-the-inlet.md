1. Left of the Beach House checkpoint, find a small underwater hole in the river.
2. Inside are four treasure chests. Open them in the right order to get the moon.

**Tip:** The order matches the Jolly Roger Bay treasure chest puzzle from Super Mario 64. A wrong chest resets the puzzle.
