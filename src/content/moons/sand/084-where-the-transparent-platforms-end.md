1. On one of the two small hills between the Odyssey and Tostarena Town, take the Moon Pipe.
2. Capture the Moe-Eye at the start to see the invisible platforms.
3. Ride the moving platform through the clear maze, avoiding walls that push you off, and jump off at the end for the moon.
