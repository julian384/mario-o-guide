1. In Tostarena Town, climb the building on the right and capture the Mini Rocket on top.
2. Cross the rotating buildings in the secret area to the end.

**Tip:** The area doesn't let you use Cappy, so jump timing is everything. Wait for each building to turn flat before landing.
