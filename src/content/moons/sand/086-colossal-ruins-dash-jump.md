1. Take the Moon Pipe at the Tostarena Ruins Entrance.
2. Ride the spark pylon to the curved road, which starts sinking into the poison.
3. Use the Rocket Flowers to run along the curved wall and jump the gaps until you reach the moon.

**Tip:** The poison here is instant death, even in Assist Mode. Don't stop moving.
