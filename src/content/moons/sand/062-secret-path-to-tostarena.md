1. This moon is reached through a painting in another kingdom.
2. If you went to the Lake Kingdom first, the painting is in a small pool in Lake Lamode's Courtyard. If you went to the Wooded Kingdom first, it's behind an iron block on the iron wall, reached by walking along the top of the wall from the Station 8 flag.
3. Jump in to land on a floating island above Tostarena and grab the moon.
