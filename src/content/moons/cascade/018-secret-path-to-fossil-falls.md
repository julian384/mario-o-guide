1. This moon is reached through a painting in another kingdom.
2. If you went to the Snow Kingdom first, the painting is on a high ledge at the north end of the starting area. If you went to the Seaside Kingdom first, it's inside the Glass Palace.
3. Jump into the painting to land on an island in the sky above Fossil Falls and take the moon.
