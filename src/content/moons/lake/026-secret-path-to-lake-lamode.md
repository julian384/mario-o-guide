1. This moon is reached through a painting in another kingdom. Where it is depends on your route.
2. If you did Lake before Wooded: the painting is on a high ledge at the north end of the Snow Kingdom's starting area, or inside the Glass Palace in the Seaside Kingdom, depending on which of those two kingdoms you visited first.
3. If you did Wooded before Lake: it's on a hidden ledge right behind the Odyssey in the Metro Kingdom.
4. Jump in to land on top of the Viewing Balcony and grab the moon.
