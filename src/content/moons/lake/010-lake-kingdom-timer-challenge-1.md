1. From the Underwater Entrance checkpoint, swim up to the surface and find the platform with a scarecrow.
2. Throw Cappy onto the scarecrow. A moon appears at the bottom of the lake.
3. Dive straight down and grab it before it disappears.

**Tip:** Ground pound in the water to sink faster.
