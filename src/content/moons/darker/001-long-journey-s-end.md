1. Collect 500 Power Moons to open the Darker Side, then fly there.
2. Go down the pipe into the tunnel under Culmina Crater. It's one long gauntlet of platforming and capture challenges over lava.
3. There are no checkpoints. If you lose a life you restart from the entrance to the underground section, so use the Life-Up Hearts along the way.
4. Come out on the second platform and climb to the top of the New Donk City Hall replica for the Multi Moon.

**Tip:** Clearing it also gets you the Invisibility Hat.
