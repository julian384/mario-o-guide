Collect 500 Power Moons to open the Darker Side, then fly there. It's one long run with no checkpoints: if you lose a life you go back to the Warp Pipe at the start of the underground area.

1. **Start.** Capture the frog and hop across the platforms to the Warp Pipe.
2. **Underground.** Beat or slip past the Yoofoe (beating it drops a Life-Up Heart), then cross the sinking platforms and climb the sinking poles.
3. **Lava.** Long jump across the hills moving over the lava. Capture a Lava Bubble to swim across, then hop between pools while dodging Moonsnakes.
4. **Uproot tower.** Ride the lava cannon, then capture an Uproot and climb the tower, avoiding the Burrbos and the blocks that push out of the walls.
5. **Freezing water and Yoshi.** Get past the rising and falling freezing water and the Fuzzies. Capture Yoshi and use his tongue to climb the conveyor-belt walls.
6. **Flower path.** Hit the P Switch and run along the flower path. Pulse Beams clear the rocks in your way. A side jump to a big rock leads to the Sphynx, and answering its riddle gets you a Life-Up Heart.
7. **Wall blocks.** Throw Cappy onto the scarecrow, then climb using the blocks that push out of the wall. They're the only platforms and can shove you off.
8. **Glydon.** Capture the Glydon and ride the updrafts, avoiding the Urban Stingbies.
9. **Volbonan poles.** After you land, fling yourself across the lava on the poles to the next Warp Pipe.
10. **Abyss and 8-bit.** Ride the platform with a Pulse Beam over the foggy drop, fending off Burrbos. Capture a Pokio to climb the wooden pendulums. In the 8-bit spiral, dodge the barrels and hit the four ? Blocks under Donkey Kong.
11. **Bowser.** Jump into the painting to capture Bowser. Smash through the six-layer wall, cross the two bridges with rolling boulders, push through the narrow tunnel (Bowser can't jump there), then climb the pyramid past seven boulder launchers. Drop through the dip in the last wall.
12. **Finish.** Ride the spark pylons that spell "THANK YOU" and take the last Warp Pipe. On the surface, capture one of the two frogs and climb the gray New Donk City Hall to the Multi Moon at the top.

**Tip:** Clearing it also gets you the Invisibility Hat.
