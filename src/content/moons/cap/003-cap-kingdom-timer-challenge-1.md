1. Go to the base of Top-Hat Tower and find the scarecrow.
2. Throw Cappy onto the scarecrow. A moon appears on the far side of Sometimes Bridge and a timer starts.
3. Run straight across the bridge and grab the moon before the timer runs out.

**Tip:** Sometimes Bridge flips between solid and invisible. Watch its rhythm before throwing Cappy so you start on a solid beat.
