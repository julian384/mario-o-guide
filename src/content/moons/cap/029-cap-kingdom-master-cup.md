1. Collect the Regular Cup moon first.
2. Talk to the Koopa Troopa near the top of Top-Hat Tower again and enter the Master Cup.
3. Beat the faster racers to the flag by the Odyssey.

**Tip:** The Master Cup racers are much quicker. Use rolls and cap jumps the whole way and take every shortcut off the tower.
