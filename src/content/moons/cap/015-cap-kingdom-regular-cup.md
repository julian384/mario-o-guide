1. Climb near the top of Top-Hat Tower and talk to the Koopa Troopa to enter the Koopa Freerunning race.
2. Race to the finish flag near the Odyssey and come first.

**Tip:** Roll down slopes and dive-cap-bounce off the tower to cut corners. Taking the straightest line down beats following the path.
