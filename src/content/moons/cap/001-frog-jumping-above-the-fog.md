1. Capture one of the frogs near where the Odyssey lands.
2. Head to the south side of the kingdom, where small hat-shaped platforms float over the poison fog.
3. Frog-jump from platform to platform. The moon sits on the last one.

**Tip:** Hold the jump button as a frog for the full height. A regular jump falls short.
