1. Find the Coin Coffer hidden on a small hill in the Deep Woods and capture it.
2. Take it to the small sprout by the river pool.
3. Shoot coins at the sprout until it blooms. It takes 500 coins.

**Tip:** The coins come out of your own total, so have at least 500 before you start.
