1. This moon is in the Iron Cage and is reached through a painting in another kingdom.
2. If you did Wooded before Lake: the painting is on a high ledge at the north end of the Snow Kingdom's starting area, or inside the Glass Palace in the Seaside Kingdom, depending on which of those two you visited first.
3. If you did Lake before Wooded: it's on a hidden ledge right behind the Odyssey in the Metro Kingdom.
