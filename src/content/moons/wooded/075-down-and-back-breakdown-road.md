1. Near the Iron Road Entrance, take the Moon Pipe on the wall.
2. Run across the breakable blocks while Bullet Bills blow them apart.
3. Grab the key at the end and run back to the start for the moon.

**Tip:** Falling here is instant death, even in Assist Mode.
