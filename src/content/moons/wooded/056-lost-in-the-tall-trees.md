1. Spot the crate on a wooden platform on a tall pine in the eastern woods.
2. Capture the Glydon at the Observation Deck and glide over it.
3. Release the capture above the crate and ground pound it. The moon lands on a golden pole near the Summit Path.

**Tip:** A Sherm from below the Summit Path can also shoot the crate.
